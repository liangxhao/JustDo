This directory contains a browser extension derived from the OpenClaw extension
shipped with the OpenClaw v2026.9.2 runtime. __PRODUCT_NAME__ customizes the
user-facing product identity and setup flow while preserving relay protocol
compatibility.
Upstream: https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw
